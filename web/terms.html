What PermitPal is

A tool to organize your permits and registrations and remind you about deadlines. Suggested due dates and checklists are guidance based on common rules; your LGU or agency has the final say. Always confirm with the issuing office.

Your responsibilities

Keep your details accurate and your login private. You are responsible for renewing on time; reminders can be delayed or fail.

Professionals listed on PermitPal

Liaison firms, accountants and other professionals listed on PermitPal are independent businesses. When you request a quote, you deal with them directly and any work, price and payment are between you and them. Listed firms pay PermitPal: a fee per quote request we send them (from credit they buy in advance), or a flat monthly fee. Firms never pay PermitPal a share of what you pay them. A week after you request a quote we may ask you once whether you hired the firm; answering is optional and helps us keep only good firms listed. Some also pay to appear as one line, labelled "Partner", on a matching step of a renewal checklist (at most one firm per step and city). This never changes their price, and you are free to use anyone. We only share your details with a firm when you press "Request a quote", and you can turn checklist suggestions off in Settings. We check a firm's business permit (and professional licence, where one is required) before listing it, but we don't guarantee their work. Listed professionals are separate from PermitPal's own done-for-you service and Year-round care below.

For listed firms. If you pay per quote request, you buy credit in advance and each request we send you uses its agreed fee, whether or not you get the job; we email you when your credit runs low, and your listing pauses when it can't cover one more request. Credit doesn't expire. If a request is spam, a duplicate, can't be reached after reasonable tries, or isn't something you do, report it within 7 days using the link in that request's email; we decide within 2 business days and, if we accept it, put the fee back on your credit.

Firms that want to be listed apply on the "For service providers" page. The fee and how it's billed are agreed before a listing goes live. We send a statement each month for that month's quote requests, sales and listings, due within 15 days; you can stop being listed anytime, effective the end of that month. We don't accept per-request or per-sale fees from insurers unless PermitPal holds the license the Insurance Code requires for that.

Done-for-you service

For now PermitPal only takes work that can be done fully online (for example setting up your account, or a DTI business name renewal). When you ask PermitPal to renew or apply for a permit for you:

  • Quote first. We send an exact quote (our service fee plus the government fees we expect). Nothing starts and nothing is charged until you accept it.
  • Your authorization. We act only as your authorized representative, with the authorization you give in the app and a signed authorization letter. You confirm that the documents and details you give us are true and complete.
  • Government fees at cost. We pay the office's official fees for you and upload the official receipts. If the fees are lower than quoted, we refund the difference. If they would be higher, we ask you before paying.
  • No fixing. We follow each office's standard process and never pay or offer anyone anything to speed things up; that is against the law (RA 11032). Processing times are set by the government office, so turnaround times we give are estimates.
  • Refunds. If we can't get it done, you get our service fee back in full. If you cancel after paying but before we file anything, you get the service fee back; once we have filed, the service fee is not refundable, and government fees already paid to the office can't be recovered.
  • Your files. While your request is open, PermitPal staff can open the documents of that permit to do the work. Every file a staff member opens is listed in your Settings → Workspace & data.
  • Rush. If you mark a request urgent, the rush fee shown before you ask (and again in the quote) pays for our priority: we file within 1 business day and follow up daily. It doesn't change the government office's processing time.
  • Several at once. When you ask for several permits together, each one gets its own quote that you accept or decline separately.
  • "Set up my account for me". For this job, and only after you agree when accepting the quote, a PermitPal staff member joins your workspace as a team member (not an admin). You see them under Team and can remove them anytime; their access ends automatically when the job is marked done, and never lasts longer than 30 days. Everything they add or change shows in your History.
  • Packages show their steps on the order, so you can see what's done and what's next.

Other licences (for example FDA or DOH licences to operate, discharge permits, contractor licences, franchises and comprehensive insurance) and permits you added yourself are one-time jobs with their own quote. Paying restarts it from that day; you're never billed for the weeks it was paused.

On-time guarantee (All-in plan)

If a permit covered by the All-in plan (earlier called Care Plus) lapses because PermitPal missed its deadline, we pay back the late penalty or surcharge the office charged, up to the limit shown for your plan (₱10,000 per permit for a branch, ₱3,000 for a vehicle). It applies when: the plan was paid up on the permit's expiry date; the permit was in your plan at least 30 days before it expired; and you sent the documents, information and government fees we asked for within 7 days of our request. It doesn't apply to delays or closures of government offices beyond our control, changes in requirements announced after we filed, or penalties for other reasons (for example back taxes or violations found during an inspection). Send a claim in the app with the official receipt within 90 days of the expiry date; we decide within 5 business days and pay approved claims by GCash or bank transfer. This guarantee is part of our service to you, not insurance.
