Processing clock and letters

When you tell Pinoy Permit the day you handed in an application, it counts the processing time that the Ease of Doing Business law (RA 11032) or the office's Citizen's Charter allows, in working days, skipping weekends and the national holidays we know of; you can add days the office was closed. It's a guide: the law and the office's records decide. When the time runs out, we tell you and draft a follow-up letter to the office and, if you want, a complaint to the Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA). You decide whether to send them and what they say; Pinoy Permit doesn't send anything to an office or to ARTA for you, and the letters are not legal advice. The law's "deemed approved" rule applies only when complete requirements were submitted and the fees paid, and some permits (for example on health, safety or the environment) follow their own rules.
