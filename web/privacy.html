Privacy Policy

Draft — have a lawyer review before launch.

Who we are

PermitPal ("we") is the personal information controller for the data in your account. Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) handles every privacy question, request and complaint. Reach the DPO by replying to any PermitPal email or through the support contact shown in the app.

What we collect

Your name, email and mobile number (optional); your businesses, branches, vehicles and staff; permit, registration and licence details (reference numbers, issue and expiry dates, amounts paid); copies of documents you upload; and, if you pay for a team plan, payment references.

Sensitive information. Some of what you may add is sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act, such as staff driver's license, PRC license, NBI clearance and health certificate details. Add it only for staff who have agreed to it. We use it only to track those licences for you.

Why we use it

To track your compliance and remind you before anything expires (to deliver the service you signed up for), to send a quote request to a professional when you ask us to, to keep records the law requires (such as payment records), and to keep the service secure. We do not use your data for advertising and we never sell it.

Who can see it

Only people you invite to your workspace. PermitPal staff do not open your files. If you connect your workspace to another company (for example your franchisor, accountant or mall), they see the names, status and expiry dates of your permits, and copies only if you turn that on; you can disconnect anytime. A share link shows only the permit names, statuses and dates you chose, never your files.

When you request a quote from a listed professional, we send them only your name, business name, contact details, your message and, if you picked one, that permit and its expiry date. Our service providers process data for us under contract: Supabase (database and file storage), Resend (email), and PayMongo (card and e-wallet payments, if you pay online).

How long we keep it

As long as your workspace exists, so your permit history stays complete. If you delete your workspace, it is erased 30 days after the request, and encrypted backups roll off within 30 days after that. Payment records are kept as long as tax rules require.

How we protect it

Every table is locked to your workspace by database rules, files are stored privately, connections are encrypted, and backups are encrypted every night. If a breach ever puts your personal data at real risk, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of finding out.

Your rights (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

You can be informed, access your data, correct it, object to its use, ask for it to be erased or blocked, and get a copy you can take elsewhere. You can download everything anytime (Settings → Workspace & data) and delete your workspace yourself. For anything else, contact our DPO. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that affects you, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
