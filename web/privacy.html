Privacy Policy

Draft — have a lawyer review before launch.

What we collect

Your name, email, mobile number (optional), your businesses, branches and vehicles, permit and registration details (reference numbers, dates), and copies of documents you upload.

Why

To track your compliance, remind you before things expire, and — only when you ask — help you renew through PermitPal staff or partner agencies.

Who can see it

Only people you invite to your workspace. PermitPal staff can see a requirement and its files only while you have an open help request for it. We never sell your data.

Where it is stored

On Supabase (database and private file storage) with encrypted nightly backups. Reminders are sent through email, SMS and WhatsApp providers you enable.

Your rights (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

You can download your data anytime (Settings → Workspace & data), correct it, or delete your workspace. Deleted workspaces are erased 30 days after the request; encrypted backups roll off within 30 days after that.

Contact

Questions or requests: reply to any PermitPal email.
