# November 2026 brief: what was built, what wasn't, and why

Short report for the Master Implementation Brief (research baseline October 24, 2026). Database file 16
(`supabase/manual/2026-11-02-brief.sql`) switches everything on (feature `lgus`).

## 1. Audit of the existing app

| Area | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Home / status | Status tiles + Next actions (real data) | Kept. "Do this first" replaced by a month calendar with progress beside it; a deadline opens that permit's renewal; an empty day adds a to-do |

## 2. Pricing clarity (launch-blocking items)

- Banner directly above the plan cards: "SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTIONS = TRACKING & PLANNING ONLY. Hands-on renewal services are optional and cost extra."
- Every plan card says "Tracking and planning software only." The pricing page headline and supporting copy are the brief's.
- "A little peace of mind" band under the plans: the lowest per-vehicle figure (₱33.33, Fleet Pro), worked out from the database prices, with every qualifier from the brief.
- "Need us to handle the renewal?": Renewal Assistance ₱499 (available: it is the papers check), Full-Service Vehicle Renewal from ₱1,000 (coming soon, no buy button), Complex case (custom quote). The brief's full wording is under "Exactly what's included".
- A short version on Home, each vehicle page, Done for you and Settings > Plan.

## 3. Not built, on purpose

- **In-person work** (Tuesday/Thursday bill runs at ₱750, Full-Service filing, runners, couriers of originals): the founder chose to stay online-only for now. Shown as "coming soon".
- **Provider verification workflow and provider records**: no verified providers exist yet; nothing is invented.
- **Bulk import/export, role permissions and fleet reports as plan promises**: spreadsheet import and export already exist; nothing new is promised.
- **Two-way Google Calendar sync** (needs Google sign-in and app review). Built instead: a private calendar link Google Calendar subscribes to, and one-tap "add to Google" for each to-do.

## 4. Metro Manila register (17 LGUs)

Coverage exists for all 17; that is not the same as verified. Online payment is marked "yes" only where an official page says so.

| Status | Cities |
|---|---|
| Verified baseline | Quezon City, Pasig, Taguig, Mandaluyong, Pasay, Malabon, Muntinlupa |
| Partial | Manila, Las Piñas, Caloocan, Navotas |
| Needs official confirmation | Makati, Marikina, Parañaque, Valenzuela, San Juan, Pateros (no official link yet) |
| Online payment confirmed | Quezon City (QC E-Services), Pasay (eODB), Malabon (LandBank online) |
| Online payment reported, not confirmed | Manila (Go Manila app), Makati (MAKA-Connect), Taguig (city portal): news reports only |
